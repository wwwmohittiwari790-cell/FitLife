⚖️ BMI Calculator

What is BMI?

BMI = weight (kg) / height (m)². Standard adult screening ranges: underweight below 18.5, typical range 18.5–24.9, overweight 25–29.9, obesity 30+. BMI is only a screening tool; it does not measure body fat or diagnose health.

🥗 Nutrition Guide

Vegetarian ideas

Breakfast: oats, milk or soy milk, fruit.
Lunch: dal, roti, sabzi, curd.
Snack: roasted chana or nuts.
Dinner: paneer/tofu, rice, vegetables.

Non-vegetarian ideas

Breakfast: eggs, wholegrain toast, fruit.
Lunch: chicken or fish, rice, vegetables.
Snack: yogurt and fruit.
Dinner: eggs/fish, roti, salad.

Everyday basics

Include varied protein foods, fruit, vegetables, whole grains and adequate fluids. Portions depend on your individual needs. Avoid crash diets and unnecessary supplements.
